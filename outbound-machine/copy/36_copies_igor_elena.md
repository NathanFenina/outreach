## I-04 · UltB · méthode R.E.P.L.Y. · The R.E.P.L.Y. Method
Pattern of Reading: Sandwich (top, bottom + circling in the middle)

Relevant results. What outcomes have you helped similar companies achieve?
Empathy. How does this align with my priorities or the problems I might be experiencing?
Personalization. How does this relate to me, my department, and/or my company?
Laser-focus. In the fewest words possible (80 or less)—what is the single action you would like me to take?
You-oriented. Are you more focused on helping me or talking about how awesome you are?

## I-09 · UltB · méthode R.E.P.L.Y. · Change up your CTAs -> Low friction + Asking for permission to share Loom
Not getting replies to your emails? Don’t panic. Before you scrap the entire email, try changing up your ask (call-to-action) at the end of the email.

Gong’s data shows that interest-based CTAs outperform specific and open-ended CTAs by 2x. In other words, asking prospects for their interest is more effective than asking for their time.

Open-Ended CTA
High Friction

Low Friction
